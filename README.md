## 🙋‍♂️ About Me

I'm an **accountant turned data analyst**. For years I made sure the numbers were right. Now I turn financial and business data into **clear dashboards and decisions**.

My accounting background shapes how I work with data: I **reconcile before I report**, I question figures that look too good (or too bad), and I build reports that managers can act on, not just look at.

---

## 💼 What I Do

- 📈 **Build interactive dashboards** in Power BI that show what is happening, why, and what it costs
- 🧹 **Clean and prepare messy data** with Power Query, SQL and Excel
- 🧱 **Design data models** (star schema) that keep reports fast and accurate
- 🧮 **Write DAX measures** for KPIs, growth rates, churn, retention and revenue at risk
- ✅ **Validate every number** against the source before it reaches a report
- 💡 **Turn analysis into recommendations** that business teams can use
